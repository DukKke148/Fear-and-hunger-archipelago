# Fear & Hunger

## What does randomization do to this game?

Nothing you find in the dungeons is yours until the multiworld unlocks it. Weapons, armors, consumables,
books and skill scrolls are **unlocks**: when another player finds one of yours, it starts to appear in your
dungeon instead of going straight to your inventory.

- Weapons, armors and skill scrolls spawn once per run in a free spot (furniture, chest, item on the floor or
  boss loot) of your current part of the dungeon or deeper.
- Consumables and books join the normal loot rolls.
- Anything not unlocked yet is replaced by a basic item (simple food, cloth, herbs, stones...).
- Strong weapons that the game hands out for a price (Claymore, Miasma, Bone shears, Blue Sin) only come from
  their original event, for that same price, once unlocked.
- Floors stay random every run, and every new game is a new run: your inventory resets, checks and unlocks stay.

## What is a check?

- Entering each zone for the first time.
- Beating each creature for the first time (bestiary), each boss, and each enemy group.
- Picking up each key item.
- Reaching each ending (A, B, C, D, E, S) and ending S with each of the four starting characters.
- Learning each of 20 skills, with its scroll or at a Hexen table.

## Other features

- Access items (Access: Part 2, Part 3, Ma'habre, End, or one per floor) gate the deeper parts of the dungeon.
- Optional traps (bleeding, poison, infection, parasites, phobia, hunger, fear, torch out) and Death Link.
- Optional enemy and weapon shuffles.

## What is the goal?

Reach a number of endings, or specific endings (for example A and S).
